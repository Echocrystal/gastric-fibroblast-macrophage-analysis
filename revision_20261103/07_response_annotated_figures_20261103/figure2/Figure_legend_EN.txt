Figure. Treatment response and established biomarkers within the four frozen ecological configurations in Kim2018. A, Patient-level annotation map: each column represents the same patient across rows; columns are grouped by configuration and ordered within groups by response, MSI, EBV, CPS and patient identifier. Orange filled triangles denote responders (CR/PR); blue open circles denote non-responders (SD/PD). Dark and light cells indicate yes and no for the row-labelled MSI-positive, EBV ISH-positive and joint MSS/EBV-negative criteria. The CPS row reports observed numeric values, with an en dash for missing values. Under each configuration, biomarker-positive counts are shown relative to all group members, followed by responders/biomarker-positive patients where that subgroup exists. B, Observed response proportions in all patients (filled circles) and the nested restriction to MSS and EBV ISH-negative patients (open diamonds), with descriptive Wilson 95% binomial confidence intervals and responders/total counts. The restricted population contains 34 patients and two responders. C, All 41 available CPS values, using the same response symbols as A; black bars show medians and interquartile ranges. Deterministic vertical offsets separate identical values without changing CPS. Missing CPS counts are given separately for responders and non-responders; no missing value is imputed. All panels retain the original configuration order. These are descriptive distributions, with no new hypothesis tests; they do not establish biomarker-independent response prediction or treatment benefit.
