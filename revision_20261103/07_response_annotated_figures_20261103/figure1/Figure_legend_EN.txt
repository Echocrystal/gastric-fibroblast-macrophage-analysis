Figure 1. Relationships of the ecological, sender and receiver scores with established clinical biomarkers in Kim2018, annotated by anti-PD-1 response. Rows show the fixed ecological, fibroblast sender and macrophage receiver scores (within-cohort Z scores), respectively. A, D and G compare MSI-positive (n = 6) with microsatellite-stable (MSS; n = 39) tumours according to the pentaplex assay. B, E and H compare EBV in situ hybridisation-positive (n = 5) with EBV-negative (n = 40) tumours. Boxes indicate the median and first and third quartiles, whiskers extend to the most extreme values within 1.5 interquartile ranges, and all individual patients are shown. Orange filled triangles indicate responders (CR or PR; 12 of 45 patients) and blue open circles indicate non-responders (SD or PD; 33 of 45); response symbols are consistent across every panel. R in the group labels denotes the number of responders. Both biomarker-status groups use the same pale neutral-grey boxes; the x-axis labels identify the clinical groups, whereas point colour and shape identify treatment response. Δmedian denotes the positive-minus-negative group median difference. C, F and I show score relationships with PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS), available for 41 of 45 patients; four patients with missing CPS were omitted only from these three panels (11 responders and 30 non-responders have observed CPS; one responder and three non-responders have missing CPS). For visual separation of the many low CPS values, CPS is displayed on a log1p scale with tick labels in original CPS units; the scores and statistical tests use the original data. No fitted line or prediction model is shown. Binary comparisons use two-sided Mann-Whitney U tests with asymptotic tie correction and continuity correction. CPS associations use two-sided Spearman rank correlations with average ranks for ties and asymptotic P values. Nominal P values and Benjamini-Hochberg q values adjusted across all nine comparisons are annotated; these are unchanged from the previous all-patient analysis, not response-stratified tests. Response colour and shape provide an additional descriptive overlay only; no new response-stratified hypothesis tests were performed. Every point represents one independent patient. This post hoc analysis describes relationships with existing biomarkers; it does not establish independent response prediction, treatment benefit or absence of a relationship.
