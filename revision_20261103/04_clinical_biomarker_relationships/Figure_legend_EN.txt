Figure. Relationships of the ecological, sender and receiver scores with established clinical biomarkers in Kim2018. Rows show the fixed ecological, fibroblast sender and macrophage receiver scores (within-cohort Z scores), respectively. A, D and G compare MSI-positive (n = 6) with microsatellite-stable (MSS; n = 39) tumours according to the pentaplex assay. B, E and H compare EBV in situ hybridisation-positive (n = 5) with EBV-negative (n = 40) tumours. Boxes indicate the median and first and third quartiles, whiskers extend to the most extreme values within 1.5 interquartile ranges, and all individual patients are shown. Δmedian denotes the positive-minus-negative group median difference. C, F and I show score relationships with PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS), available for 41 of 45 patients; four patients with missing CPS were omitted only from these three panels. For visual separation of the many low CPS values, CPS is displayed on a log1p scale with tick labels in original CPS units; the scores and statistical tests use the original data. No fitted line or prediction model is shown. Binary comparisons use two-sided Mann-Whitney U tests with asymptotic tie correction and continuity correction. CPS associations use two-sided Spearman rank correlations with average ranks for ties and asymptotic P values. Nominal P values and Benjamini-Hochberg q values adjusted across all nine comparisons are annotated. Every point represents one independent patient. This post hoc analysis describes relationships with existing biomarkers; it does not establish independent response prediction, treatment benefit or absence of a relationship.
