Figure. Established biomarker distributions across ecological configurations in the Kim2018 anti-PD-1 cohort. A, Observed response proportions in the four frozen, outcome-independent median-defined configurations; points show responders/total patients and whiskers show descriptive Wilson 95% binomial confidence intervals. B, Pentaplex MSI status, classified as MSI or microsatellite stable (MSS). C, EBV status by in situ hybridisation, classified as positive or negative. Bar labels report counts and configuration-specific denominators; MSI and EBV-ISH status were complete for all 45 patients. D, Individual available PD-L1 combined positive scores (CPS; n = 41) with black median and interquartile-range summaries. Identical CPS values are offset vertically for visibility without changing their values. Four missing CPS measurements are explicitly counted and are not classified as zero or negative. No between-group hypothesis tests were performed. These descriptive distributions contextualise confounding by established biomarkers in a small single-arm cohort and do not establish independent predictive performance or treatment benefit. Configuration colours follow the original Figure 6.
